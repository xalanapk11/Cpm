Frequently Asked
What is CPM1 / CPM2? ▾
CPM1 = Car Parking Multiplayer 1 (older version). CPM2 = Car Parking Multiplayer 2 (new version). Different features apply to each game.
What is an ES3 file? ▾
ES3 is the save file format used by Car Parking Multiplayer. You can export it from the game's settings and upload it here for decryption or modification.
How do I farm coins? ▾
Go to Features → Coins Farming. Enter your CPM2 email and password, choose farming mode (MAX / DRAG / COMBO / BOTH), and the bot will farm automatically. Results are sent to your Telegram chat.
What does VIP Vinyl Transfer do? ▾
It moves a VIP car's vinyl (custom paint/design) from one account to another. Useful for keeping a design on a new account.
How do I buy from the Store? ▾
Tap the Store tab, choose a package, and follow the steps. You'll pay with Telegram Stars or USDT (Binance Pay) and get your account credentials sent to your chat.
My run limit says 0 / 0 — what to do? ▾
Contact @imal4n on Telegram to top up your lifetime run quota. Your access may have expired or never been granted.
I got an error — who do I contact? ▾
Message @imal4n directly on Telegram with a screenshot of the error message. Include your Telegram ID if possible.
